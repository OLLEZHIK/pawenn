## Your pet had an encounter with a bat or wild animal

In Los Angeles County, bats are the primary carrier of rabies. If your pet had contact with a bat, raccoon, skunk, or coyote, report the incident immediately to LA County Veterinary Public Health. Pets with current rabies vaccinations must undergo a **30-day quarantine**, while unvaccinated pets face a **180-day quarantine** or euthanasia.

## Your dog bit someone

Any dog that bites a person in Los Angeles must be placed under a mandatory **10-day quarantine** to monitor for signs of rabies. For healthy household pets, this quarantine is usually completed at home. Do not vaccinate the animal during the observation period.
