## Your pet met a bat or wild animal

In Los Angeles County, bats are the primary wildlife carriers of rabies. If your dog or cat catches, bites, or contacts a bat or wild mammal, contact your veterinarian immediately and report the incident to the Los Angeles County Department of Public Health (Veterinary Public Health). Never handle a bat with bare hands.

## Your dog bit someone

All mammal bites to humans must be reported to Public Health. The biting dog or cat is placed under a **10-day quarantine and observation** period to ensure it was not shedding the rabies virus. If the animal stays healthy throughout the 10 days, the person bitten does not need post-exposure rabies shots.
