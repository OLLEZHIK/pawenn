A full groom is the most comprehensive grooming service for your dog, including a bath, blow dry, haircut, styling, ear cleaning, and nail trim. Below is what Miami salons in our catalogue charge, based on their published price lists.

::price GROOMING full_groom

The rate shown is the starting price for small breeds with manageable coats. Most salons increase rates for medium and large dogs, matted hair, or scissor-finish cuts.

## A bath without a haircut

If your dog only requires washing, blow-drying, and thorough brushing, a bath service is significantly cheaper than a full haircut.

Not all pet salons accept cats. Salons that handle felines price cat grooming separately due to specialized handling requirements.
