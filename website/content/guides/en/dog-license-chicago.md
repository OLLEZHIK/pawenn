Every dog four months of age or older kept in Chicago must be registered with the Office of the City Clerk under Municipal Code 07-12-140. The registration tag must be attached to the dog's collar or harness in public.

## How much is a dog license in Chicago?

- **$5 a year** (or **$15 for three years**) for a sterilized (spayed or neutered) dog.
- **$2.50 a year** (or **$7.50 for three years**) for sterilized dogs owned by seniors aged 65 and older.
- **$50 a year** (or **$150 for three years**) for an unsterilized dog ($5 a year or $15 for three years for seniors).

License terms run for one or three years to match the duration of the dog's rabies vaccination. Failing to register a dog carries a fine of **$30 to $200**, and each day the violation continues counts as a separate offense.

## How to apply

Apply online through the City Clerk's EzBuy portal, or visit a City Clerk office location in person. You need your dog's rabies vaccination certificate number and expiration date from a licensed veterinarian. Online tags arrive by mail within **12 business days**.

Each registration emblem carries a unique number. If your dog is lost, anyone who finds it can call **312-744-DOGS** to help locate you.

## Rabies vaccination

Cook County and Illinois law require every dog to be vaccinated against rabies. Vaccines are administered by licensed veterinarians, with one-year and three-year formulations available. Proof of rabies immunization is required for city licensing, boarding facilities, and park permits.

## Leash laws and Dog Friendly Areas

Under Chicago Municipal Code 7-12-030, dogs outside private property must be kept on a leash at all times. Violating the leash law carries a fine starting at **$300**. Owners who fail to clean up after their dogs face fines of up to **$500** (Municipal Code 7-12-420).

The only places where dogs can legally exercise off-leash are the Chicago Park District's **34 Dog Friendly Areas (DFAs)**, including Belmont Harbor, Montrose Beach, Fred Anderson, and Mary Bartelme. To enter a DFA, your dog needs an annual Park District DFA permit and tag, which costs **$10 per dog** (valid January 1 through December 31). DFA permits are issued through participating veterinary clinics and require a city dog license or rabies proof, a negative parasite fecal test within 12 months, and vaccines for DHPP, Bordetella, and Leptospirosis. Using a DFA without a permit carries a fine of up to **$500**.

## Vets and trainers in Chicago

The rabies shot and DFA permit health exams are provided by licensed vet clinics.
