Dog walkers visit your home to give your dog exercise during the day, while daycare centers and pet hotels provide boarding and supervision away from home. Here is what services cost in Los Angeles.

## 30-minute dog walk

::price PET_SITTING walk_30

A standard 30-minute visit includes a walk, fresh water, and feeding if needed. Rates may vary between solo walks and small group walks.

## One-hour dog walk

::price PET_SITTING walk_60

A 60-minute walk provides longer exercise or neighborhood socialization. Sitter profiles note whether recurring weekly schedules receive discounted rates.

## A day at doggy daycare

Daycare centers host dogs during work hours with supervised indoor and outdoor play areas.

::price PET_HOTEL daycare_day

Most facilities require a temperament evaluation before your dog's first day and proof of current vaccinations.

## Overnight dog boarding

Pet hotels charge per night for boarding, with accommodations ranging from standard suites to luxury private rooms.
