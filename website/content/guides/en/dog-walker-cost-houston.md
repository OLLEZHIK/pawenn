Dog walkers provide mid-day exercise and companionship for your dog while you work, while doggy daycare centers and pet hotels offer group play and overnight boarding across Houston. Here is what services cost based on local published rates.

## 30-minute dog walk

A 30-minute private dog walk gives your dog exercise, outdoor relief, fresh water, and attention right in your neighborhood.

::price PET_SITTING walk_30

Many professional sitters offer discounts for recurring Monday-through-Friday walking schedules or charge small surcharges for additional household dogs.

## A day at doggy daycare

Daycare centers supervise dogs in indoor and outdoor pack play areas during work hours.

::price PET_HOTEL daycare_day

Daycare operators typically require a temperament evaluation on your dog's first visit, along with proof of up-to-date vaccinations (rabies, distemper/parvo, and bordetella).

## Overnight dog boarding

Pet hotels and boarding facilities charge per night for care, ranging from standard kennel accommodations to luxury private boarding suites.

::price PET_HOTEL dog_night

Rates often include group play sessions or outdoor exercise times. Check whether special medication administration or holiday booking carries an additional fee.

All prices come directly from published price menus on local sitters' and pet hotels' official websites. Hourly sitting rates or unbundled fees are excluded from city median calculations.

While 60-minute walks are offered by Houston sitters, fewer than three providers publish fixed 60-minute rates online, so that duration does not currently have a citywide median comparison. Confirm holiday availability and intake requirements with each provider prior to booking.
