A full groom in Los Angeles is the most comprehensive grooming service, and pricing varies widely by salon and dog breed. Below is what salons in our catalogue charge, drawn directly from their published price lists.

::price GROOMING full_groom

The figure reflects the starting price for a small dog with a coat in good condition. Salons charge more for larger breeds, matting, or custom styling.

## A bath without a haircut

If your dog only needs washing, blow-drying, and brush-out, book a bath service. It costs less than a full haircut at salons that offer both options.

::price GROOMING bath_dry

## Cats

Fewer salons take cats, and those that do price cat grooming as a distinct service due to the specialized handling required.

::price GROOMING cat_groom

## Nail trim

A standalone nail trim or grinding is the quickest and least expensive salon visit.
