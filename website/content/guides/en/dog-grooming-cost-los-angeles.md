A full groom is the most comprehensive grooming service for your dog, including a bath, blow dry, haircut, styling, ear cleaning, and nail trim. Below is what Los Angeles salons in our catalogue charge, based on their published price lists.

::price GROOMING full_groom

The rate shown is the starting price for the smallest dog breeds with manageable coats. Most salons increase rates for medium and large dogs, matted hair, or specialized breed cuts.

## A bath without a haircut

If your dog only requires washing, blow-drying, and brushing out, a bath service is significantly cheaper than a full haircut.

Not all pet salons accept cats. Those that offer cat services price feline grooming separately due to specialized handling requirements.
