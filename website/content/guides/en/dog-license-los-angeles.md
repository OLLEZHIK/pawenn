Every dog over four months of age kept in the City of Los Angeles must be licensed (LAMC 53.28) and spayed or neutered (LAMC 53.15.2). The license tag must be worn on the dog's collar or harness whenever off the owner's premises.

## How much is a dog license in Los Angeles?

- **$20 a year** for a spayed or neutered dog. A three-year license is **$55**.
- **$100 a year** for an unaltered dog if exempt from the mandatory spay and neuter ordinance.
- **$10 a year** for low-income seniors aged 62 and older, or low-income disabled residents (limited to one dog per household).
- A replacement tag costs **$5**.

## How to apply

Apply online through the LA Animal Services licensing portal, by mail, or in person at any City of Los Angeles animal shelter. You must submit a copy of your dog's current rabies vaccination certificate and proof of sterilization.

A license remains valid only as long as the rabies vaccination certificate is effective. Under LAMC 53.06.1, households are limited to keeping no more than three dogs over four months of age at a single property.

## Rabies shot

All dogs four months and older must receive a rabies vaccination from a licensed veterinarian. Puppies receive their first dose at 4 months, a booster one year later, and follow-up boosters every three years.

## Leash laws and off-leash dog parks

Under LAMC 53.06.2, dogs in public places must be held on a substantial leash **no longer than six feet** by a competent person.

Dogs may run off-leash only inside designated, fenced dog parks operated by the City of Los Angeles Department of Recreation and Parks. Dogs must remain on leash in parking lots and when entering or exiting dog parks.

## Vets and trainers in Los Angeles

The rabies shot is given at a veterinary clinic.
