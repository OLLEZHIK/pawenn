Dog walkers visit your home to provide daily exercise and potty breaks, while daycare centers and pet hotels offer dedicated supervision and overnight boarding. Here is what pet sitting and lodging cost across Miami.

## 30-minute dog walk

::price PET_SITTING walk_30

A standard 30-minute visit includes a leashed walk, fresh water refill, and feeding if requested. Rates reflect single solo visits; sitters may discount regular recurring schedules.

## One-hour dog walk

::price PET_SITTING walk_60

A 60-minute walk provides longer outdoor exercise or neighborhood trail walking. In South Florida's heat, walkers often schedule longer outings during cooler morning or evening hours.

## A day at doggy daycare

Daycare centers supervise dogs throughout work hours in air-conditioned indoor playrooms and fenced outdoor yards.

::price PET_HOTEL daycare_day

Most facilities require an initial behavioral evaluation and up-to-date rabies, distemper, and bordetella vaccinations before admission.

## Overnight dog boarding

Pet hotels charge per night for boarding, with accommodations ranging from standard suites to luxury private rooms with webcams.
