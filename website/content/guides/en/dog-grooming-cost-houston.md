A full groom provides complete care for your dog, including a warm bath, blow dry, haircut, brush out, ear cleaning, and nail trim. Below is what Houston salons in our catalogue charge, based on their published price lists.

The rate shown is the base price for small breeds with manageable coats. Most salons increase prices for medium and large dogs, long coats, heavy matting, or breed-specific scissor styling.

## A bath without a haircut

For short-haired dogs or between haircuts, a bath and dry service cleans the coat and skin at a lower cost than a full groom.

## How we checked

All prices come directly from published price lists on local pet salons' official websites. Hourly rates, variable mobile travel surcharges, or unbundled packages are excluded from the citywide median calculations.

While cat grooming is offered by some pet salons in Houston, only two salons in our catalogue publish fixed feline prices online, so cat grooming does not currently have a citywide median comparison. Always confirm vaccination requirements (such as current rabies and bordetella) and cancellation policies before your appointment.
