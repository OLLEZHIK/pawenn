Many New York trainers sell packages of several sessions. Here is what a puppy course costs:

Compare what a package includes before you compare prices. In the price lists we checked, puppy courses run from four to seven sessions, and some are group classes while others are private sessions at your home.

## Private lessons

A private lesson is one trainer working with you and your dog.
