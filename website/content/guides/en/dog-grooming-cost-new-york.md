A full groom in New York is the most expensive thing a salon does for your dog, and the price swings a lot from one salon to the next. Below is what salons in our catalogue charge, taken from their own price lists.

::price GROOMING full_groom

The figure is the "from" price: the smallest dog with an easy coat. Salons that list sizes charge more as the dog gets bigger, and some quote before sales tax.

## A bath without a haircut

If your dog only needs washing, drying and brushing, ask for a bath. It costs less than a full groom at every salon that lists both.

::price GROOMING bath_dry

## Cats

Fewer salons list cat grooming. Those that do price a cat groom or a cat bath as a separate service.

::price GROOMING cat_groom

## Nail trim

A nail trim on its own is the cheapest visit.

## How we checked

Every price comes from the salon's own website, on the date shown with the price. A price per hour or a price that leaves part of the service out is listed on the salon's page but not counted in the median. Prices change, so confirm when you book.
