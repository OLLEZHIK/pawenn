# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 4, 2026*

This policy explains what Pawenn (`pawenn.com`, "we") does with data when you use the site. In short: no accounts, no ads, no tracking across other sites.

## Who we are

Pawenn is a free directory of pet services. Contact for anything in this policy: `{EMAIL}`.

## What we process

**Listings.** The directory shows public business information (name, address, phone, website, opening hours, prices) taken from the businesses' own websites and public records. A business can ask us to correct or remove its listing at any time at `{EMAIL}`.

**Reviews you write.** If you submit a review, we store the name you enter, your rating, your comment and the date, and show them on the listing. Please do not put anything private in a review. To have a review removed, write to `{EMAIL}`.

**Clicks on listings.** When you tap call, website, route or email on a listing, we record the type of click, the listing, the page it came from and the time. We do not record your IP address and do not set an identifier.

**Analytics.** We use Vercel Web Analytics, which sets no cookies. It counts page views with approximate location (country, region, city), device, browser and referring site. We see only aggregated statistics, not individual visitors.

**Language cookie.** If you switch the language, we store one cookie, `pawenn_lang`, with the language code, for one year. It identifies no one. Visiting the site sets no other cookie.

**Your location.** "Nearest to me" asks your browser for your location. It is used in your browser to find the nearest city and is not sent to or stored by us.

## Why, and on what basis

To run the directory, keep it accurate and measure how it is used (legitimate interest, GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)); to publish a review you choose to send (consent, Art. 6(1)(a)).

## Who else handles data

Our hosting and infrastructure providers process data on our behalf: Vercel (hosting, analytics), Cloudflare (network and security) and Neon (database). We do not sell or rent data.

## How long we keep it

Aggregated click statistics are kept without time limit, as they identify no one. Reviews stay on the listing until you or the business ask us to remove them or the listing is removed.

## Your rights

You can ask us for access to, correction or deletion of your data, object to processing, or withdraw consent, by writing to `{EMAIL}`. You can also complain to your national data protection authority.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.
